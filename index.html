Dr. Anagha P

Postdoctoral Research Associate

I am Anagha P., a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the University of Calgary, Canada. My research focuses on advanced machine learning techniques, particularly Graph Neural Networks (GNNs), and their applications in hydrology, hydrological modelling, climate change impacts, and water resources management. I am passionate about advancing scientific knowledge through data analysis, computational modelling, field research, and interdisciplinary collaboration with experts from diverse domains. Currently, my work at the University of Calgary involves developing Graph Neural Network based models to improve the calibration and regionalization of hydrological models, with the goal of enhancing streamflow prediction accuracy.

This website includes my academic background, selected publications, and contact information.
